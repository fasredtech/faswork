# FASWORK — Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. What is FASWORK?

FASWORK is a maintenance lifecycle management platform being developed by FASRED Technologies Ltd. It is designed to help organisations manage assets, coordinate maintenance activities, control resources and costs, and improve operational reliability.

## 2. What problems is FASWORK designed to solve?

FASWORK aims to help organisations reduce fragmented maintenance records, improve visibility into asset condition, coordinate maintenance work, track resources and expenditure, and make maintenance decisions more traceable and accountable.

## 3. Who is FASWORK designed for?

FASWORK is intended for organisations of different sizes, from small workshops and single-site institutions to large enterprises and multi-location organisations.

Potential users include maintenance teams, facility managers, engineers, operations teams, asset managers, procurement teams, contractors, and authorised management personnel.

## 4. Which industries can use FASWORK?

The platform is intended to support a broad range of operating environments, including:

- Healthcare and hospitals.
- Schools, universities, and campuses.
- Manufacturing and industrial operations.
- Construction and facilities management.
- Energy, utilities, and infrastructure.
- Transport, logistics, and fleet operations.
- Agriculture and food production.
- Government and public institutions.
- Hospitality, retail, and commercial property.
- Laboratories and research facilities.

Specific workflows may need to be configured for each organisation and sector.

## 5. What types of maintenance does FASWORK address?

Its intended scope includes corrective, preventive, emergency, condition-based, predictive, inspection, statutory, reliability-focused, overhaul, contractor-managed, calibration, testing, and other maintenance activities appropriate to the organisation.

The availability of individual maintenance strategies depends on the implemented product capabilities.

## 6. Does FASWORK manage the complete maintenance lifecycle?

FASWORK is being developed around an end-to-end lifecycle covering asset context, problem reporting, triage, diagnosis, risk assessment, planning, authorisation, resource preparation, execution, verification, acceptance, return to service, reconciliation, closure, and continuous improvement.

The actual workflow should depend on the type of maintenance, its risk, and the organisation's requirements.

## 7. Does marking a work order as completed mean the maintenance is finished?

Not necessarily. The governing product principle is that a status change alone should not replace the evidence and approvals required for a particular job.

Where applicable, completion may require documented work, successful verification, acceptance, appropriate asset service status, and reconciliation of materials and costs.

## 8. Can FASWORK manage assets across multiple locations?

Multi-location asset and maintenance management is part of the product direction. The intended approach supports local operational responsibilities alongside appropriate wider organisational oversight.

The precise capabilities available depend on the implementation and release.

## 9. Can different teams have different permissions?

FASWORK is intended to support role-based permissions and organisational responsibilities so that users can access the information and actions appropriate to their roles and scope.

Actual permission options depend on the released implementation.

## 10. Can FASWORK manage spare parts, procurement, and maintenance costs?

The product's intended scope includes materials, inventory, tools, procurement, suppliers, labour, contractors, budgets, invoices, and cost reconciliation.

These records should distinguish events such as requesting materials, approving purchases, receiving goods, consuming stock, approving invoices, and confirming payment.

## 11. Does FASWORK support safety checks and maintenance verification?

The product is designed to support applicable safety preparations, documented checks, technical verification, acceptance, and controlled return-to-service decisions.

FASWORK is a management tool; it does not replace qualified professional judgement, required inspections, applicable regulations, or an organisation's authorised safety procedures.

## 12. Can FASWORK integrate with other business systems?

Potential integrations include identity and access management, finance and ERP systems, procurement, inventory, supplier systems, communications services, condition-monitoring systems, document management, and reporting platforms.

Specific integrations should be confirmed against the capabilities available in the relevant release.

## 13. Does FASWORK use predictive maintenance or artificial intelligence?

Predictive maintenance and AI-assisted analysis are part of the potential product direction where suitable data, validated methods, and appropriate integrations are available.

Recommendations should be evidence-based and subject to suitable human review. No predictive capability should be assumed available without confirmation.

FASWORK is under development. Contact FASRED Technologies Ltd to confirm current availability, supported capabilities, demonstrations, and pilot opportunities.
